# Shared media recorder isolation

Separate application databases may observe the same native media fleet. Foreign
work is capacity evidence only; it never becomes a business room, recording asset,
join/stop/import/delete operation, or settlement claim in this application.

## Accounting and placement

There is no enforceable foreign attendance/duration/resource contract. Withhold
whole affected media and recorder workers rather than inventing a fractional cost.
Bracket the debug room-name mapping with exact SID inventories; obtain recorder
placement independently from same-incarnation logs and process/storage evidence.
Unknown placement, incomplete footprints and conflicting incarnations hold new
pool authority. Existing protected claims, deadlines and one-shot grants survive.

Persist foreign EG/SID/media/recorder/container/start bindings in the existing
fenced capacity-control document. Active-list disappearance does not erase them:
keep those workers held through upload/cleanup until exact physical evidence proves
cleanup. A reused room name cannot move the old binding onto another incarnation.
Verified peers remain usable during finalization. This ledger supplies no foreign
ownership or recording-success authority.

## Recorder-only exclusion

The existing watcher receives a durable, incarnation-bound EXCLUDE intent under
the same allocation fence used for owned recording grants. Unresolved accepted
owned starts prevent new isolation intents. Root writes and fsyncs an intent into
the original lifetime startup-lock inode, sets only that recorder's restart policy
to `no`, then sends that exact recorder one SIGQUIT. It never signals media, kills
handlers, waits for the recorder's lifetime lock, or retries an uncertain signal.
A competing guarded launch sees the persistent nonempty barrier after acquiring
the lifetime lock. Partial/unknown/replaced markers remain held; a journal-only
crash can recover the exact original empty inode while its lifetime lock is held.

A successful signal or disabled/terminating metrics alone is insufficient. Publish
exclusion only after endpoint-owner-bound disabled/terminating/nonaccepting metrics
and the root service's post-deregistration `draining` acknowledgement, bound to its
service ID and process birth. Parse the pinned console/tab and JSON framing;
handler logs and stale/unknown messages cannot acknowledge the root service.
Continue complete physical job accounting while existing handlers finish. The
whole affected worker stays withheld, including known backup/finalization data.
Requests accepted just before deregistration can finish there; restoration cannot
begin while that service or its old owned recording obligations remain unresolved.

Once every affected recorder has positive exclusion proof, owned recorded classes
may use verified peers. Media affinity never selects an egress worker. Foreign
work alone creates no scaling demand; normal bounded owned waiting demand may
provision peers after exclusion. Held workers remain ineligible for scale-in.

## Safe automatic return and owned settlement

Wait for exact clean exit, no writers/open/deleted files, empty backup storage and
two unchanged complete storage observations at least 30 seconds apart. Preserve only
known empty handler directories and the retired service socket/directory through
the existing bounded, fsynced, same-filesystem no-replace preservation primitive.
Never delete or move recording output, unknown files, backups or active sockets.
Its archived exact plan/receipt survives interrupted renames and barrier clearing.

Existing JOB settlement can continue on the exact isolated running recorder.
The stopped recorder can publish the existing NODE storage receipt through the
same authenticated settlement APIs. Provider COMPLETE, expected output, closed
room, managed READY asset/preparation rules and exact ownership remain independently
required under lifecycle/allocation fences. Clean exit is not recording success.
Unsettled owned jobs retain the old recorder incarnation needed for that proof.

When all foreign media/recording holds and old owned recording obligations clear,
persist RESTORE with the last exclusion-authority expiry, invalidate new recording
grants during restoration, and wait that original expiry without restamping it.
Retain the maximum previously issued expiry across held frames and process restarts.
An unchanged late reporter can receive an already-persisted action without
republishing or refreshing capacity authority; changed incarnations cannot.
After durable preservation, restore only the saved recorder restart policy and
start the same container. Confirm its later StartedAt, healthy admission and new
physical footprint before returning capacity/removing the intent. Never redrain a
successfully returned incarnation. Lost acknowledgements retry the exact retained
plan; unknown bytes/data continue to hold. A foreign live-only room keeps its
recorder excluded even when the recorder has become idle.
